The problem

Neurorehabilitation doesn't scale.

12M+ new strokes worldwide each year — plus brain injury, MS and Parkinson's
~32 functional movement reps in a typical therapy session — motor-learning evidence points to 300+
  • Recovery depends on high-repetition, progressively harder practice. A therapist can't be in the room for every rep.
  • Home exercise sheets go undone. Progress stalls between visits.
  • Clinics can't prove functional gains to payers in a standardized way.

The solution

One platform for the whole recovery journey.

Adaptive therapy engine

Difficulty, assistance and pacing auto-tune to each patient — every rep stays in the productive zone.

Markerless motion capture

Clinical-grade movement tracking from a standard camera. No lab, no wearables, no markers.

Outcomes dashboard

Standardized assessment scores, range, speed and adherence — in one clinician view.

Engagement layer

Game-like tasks with clear goals and feedback, so patients keep showing up.

Clinic → home on the same prescribed program, with the care team watching the whole way.

Why now

The tailwinds just lined up.

  • Reimbursement is shifting toward remote therapeutic monitoring.
  • On-device ML + cameras are finally good enough to replace lab hardware.
  • Therapist shortages force providers to seek leverage, not more headcount.
  • Payers increasingly pay for measurable functional outcomes, not visits.
