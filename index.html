About OmniNeuro

Recovery guided by science, delivered with hope

Our mission

To make high-quality neurorehabilitation accessible to every patient who needs it — by turning the principles of neuroplasticity into practical, engaging, data-driven therapy.

Our vision

A world where a neurological diagnosis is the start of a clear recovery path, not the end of independence. We build the tools that make that path visible, motivating, and effective.

What makes our approach different

  • Adaptive by design. Therapy difficulty adjusts in real time to each patient's performance, keeping them in the productive zone of challenge.
  • Measurable outcomes. Every session generates clinician-ready metrics on range, speed, accuracy, and endurance.
  • Clinic and home continuity. The same program follows the patient from the clinic to home, so progress never stalls between visits.
  • Built with clinicians. Developed alongside physiatrists, physical and occupational therapists, and rehabilitation researchers.

Technology

The core of the OmniNeuro platform

Four connected systems that work together to drive neuroplastic change and give care teams a clear picture of recovery.

Adaptive Therapy Engine

A real-time algorithm tunes task difficulty, assistance, and pacing to each patient's moment-to-moment ability — maximizing repetitions in the optimal learning range.

Markerless Motion Capture

Standard cameras and wearable sensors track joint angles, tremor, and coordination with clinical accuracy — no bulky lab equipment or attached markers required.

Outcomes Dashboard

Clinicians see longitudinal trends, standardized assessment scores, and session adherence in one view — making it simple to adjust the plan and document progress.

Engagement Layer

Therapy is delivered as motivating, game-like activities with clear goals and feedback, so patients stay consistent through the long work of recovery.

Programs & Services

Support at every stage of recovery

Flexible programs for patients, clinics, and research partners — use them on their own or as one continuous pathway.

Clinic Program

A turnkey neurorehabilitation station for outpatient and inpatient settings. Staff training, protocol library, and integration support included.

  • Upper & lower limb, balance, and cognitive modules
  • Clinician dashboard and progress reports
  • On-site setup and staff onboarding

Home Continuity

Patients continue the same prescribed program at home on a tablet, with remote monitoring so the care team can adjust the plan between visits.

  • Prescribed sessions with reminders
  • Remote clinician oversight and messaging
  • Automatic safety flags and check-ins

Research Partnerships

Structured data exports, study configuration, and protocol customization for academic and clinical research teams investigating recovery.

  • De-identified, standardized data sets
  • Configurable outcome measures
  • Co-authored publications and IRB support
